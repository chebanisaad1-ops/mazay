Our Story

Born From a Love
of Beautiful Spaces

Founded in 2018 in Casablanca, Mazay Interiors was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to live in a space that reflects their finest self.

Our founders — architects trained in Europe with deep roots in Moroccan craftsmanship — set out to bridge the gap between global luxury design trends and the rich heritage of North African aesthetics.

Today, with over 340 completed projects across Morocco, we are the trusted partner for families, developers, and hospitality brands seeking truly extraordinary interiors.
